What still stands between the tools and a real run

Questions for Fuhao

None blocks the next step; each one turns a guess in the tools into a fact. The steps report has them in place, stage by stage.

  1. Write route. Badger routines an operator approves, or the MCP tools with approval and the safety ranges? For the gun RF and magnets, from ued-daq or through uedopr on testfac-srv01?
  2. Setup values. The taught positions (yag, andor, bi, si), the pump HWP angles for minimum energy and ~2 µJ, and the Andor through hole's pixel position and radius. The safety ranges for the pump HWP and the UV throttle are placeholders.
  3. Scope channel. The procedure text says WF_CH0_TRACE, the V/div PV given is CH2's, and the dry run confirmed CH2. Which is it? Its V/div record reads UDF: is there a readback?
  4. Emission phase. The tools take the onset, where the charge first rises 5σ above the scope noise. Onset, half-maximum or peak? Is the 30° added in the direction PREQ grows?
  5. Cameron's UV feedback loop. Where is it, is it PV-backed, and what does "keep ___" end with?
  6. Pump steering. "Pump laser mirror H and V": the run-7 list's pump lens MMN:08:05/06, or the SmarAct UED:MCS:01:m7/m8?
  7. E-beam view gain. Procedure (4) says gain 30, the state list says 10. The tools use 10.
  8. Correctors. Which PV sets the kick, BCTRL or BDES, and what is the kick per unit?
