UED bring-up procedures

Every step the ued_tuning tools take in the eight bring-up stages, with the PVs and values they use, as the code stands on 2026-10-28. They follow the 2026-10-16 dry-run notes for stages 1–3 and Fuhao's procedures for stages 4–8.

On the real machine the tools are read-only for now, so these are the steps they would take once writes are allowed. Please read for anything wrong, missing or in the wrong order.

What every stage does

Start of every stage

  1. Stages 2–8 read the four gate valves, VVPG:AS01:240, :272, :280 and :295:POSITION. They stop, before writing anything, unless all four read OPEN. Stage 1 skips this.
  2. Close the pump-laser shutter, UED:USR:BHC:AO1:3 → 0. Any step that opens it closes it again, also when the step fails.

On every write

  • The value is checked against its safety range in the setup file before it is sent. Out of range, the stage stops and nothing is written.
  • The diagnostic screen moves by command PVs: YAGS:AS01:270:OPENCMD inserts it and :CLOSECMD extracts it. :OPENSOLENOID reads its state and :INTLKSUM its interlock. Writes are disabled, so moving it is an operator step, and the stage is told when it is done.

Moving the sample stage ("go2YAG", "go2Andor", "go2Bi")

  1. Close the pump shutter.
  2. Read the taught position from the setup file: x, y, z [mm], pitch, yaw [deg]. If it isn't taught yet, stop and ask for it.
  3. Check every axis against its safety range, then write UED:USR:MMN:11:03 (x), :11:04 (y), :11:05 (z), :11:01 (pitch) and :11:08 (yaw).
  4. Wait 5 s, then read the axes back; each must be within 0.01 of its target.

Check these XPS-11 axes are from the run-7 list, but their descriptions read "Incoupling X/Y", "Outcoupling", "Capillary Y" and "Catcher Y". Is the order (x, y, z, then pitch, yaw) safe, or should something retract first?

Camera views (sample chamber camera UED:GIGE:03 and chamber light)

  • full view (state 1): exposure 0.1 s, gain 0, light on.
  • e-beam view (state 2): exposure 1 s, gain 10, light off. Check procedure (4) says gain 30, the state list says 10.
  • laser view (state 3): exposure 0.5 s, gain 5, light off.
  • The light is switched with ACSW:AS01:NW03:2POWERON / :2POWEROFF and read back from :2POWERSTATE.

Changing the Andor settings

Andor stop (UED:ANDOR:CAM:03:Acquire → 0), set :AcquireTime and :AndorEMGain, Andor start (→ 1), then wait one exposure plus 1 s.

E-beam check on the YAG (in stages 4, 6 and 8)

  1. Move the sample stage to yag, then set the e-beam view.
  2. Gun UV shutter (UED:USR:BHC:AO1:4) closed: read the camera 5 times. Then open: read 5 times. The e-beam is what appears with the shutter open, and it must make up more than half of the counts. Otherwise it is dark current only.
  3. Compare the beam centroid with the cross, UED:GIGE:03:Cross2X / Cross2Y:
    • within 5 px: on the cross;
    • within 150 px: move the cross onto the beam (stages 4 and 6; stage 8 only checks);
    • farther: stop, and an operator judges.

Check the 5 px and 150 px thresholds.

Camera centroids and sizes are computed from the image itself (IMAGE1:ArrayData; IMAGE2 on the Andor), because most of the cameras' Stats plugins are switched off.

Reading the charge on the scope

  1. Read the 500-sample trace SCOP:AS01:SC01:WF_CH2_TRACE (in divisions) and the volts/div :MBBO_CH2_SCL.
  2. Take the baseline as the median of the trace and the pulse as its largest dip below the baseline.
  3. Convert: mV = dip × V/div, then charge = mV ÷ (−0.17 mV/fC). So −1.7 mV is 10 fC.
  4. The pulse counts as detected when the dip is more than 5 times the trace noise. At 1 mV/div that is about 7 fC.

Check the procedure text names WF_CH0_TRACE; the V/div PV given and the dry run both point to CH2. The channel is a setting, CH2 now. The V/div record reads UDF: is there a readback?

STAGE 1

Gun ramp-up

tool gun_ramp · gun RF on the TS1 klystron, SIOC:AS01:KY01:0:

Before it starts

  • The operator confirms on the panel: AREQ at 0, the modulator in TRIG, HV at full voltage (VoltSet 980 V). These can't be read yet.

Steps

  1. Close the pump shutter.
  2. Read AREQ. If it is parked above 0.05 (it read 0.2 on 2026-10-21), the next step lowers it.
  3. Set AREQ → 0.05.
  4. Enable the phase and amplitude feedbacks: PFBENB, AFBENB → Enable. Wait 1 s.
  5. Both PFBST and AFBST must read Active; otherwise abort.
  6. Step AREQ from 0.05 to 0.53 in 300 steps, 2 s apart. Before each step:
    • the gun body temperature, APC:AS01:TM01:M2_CH2_SPARE, must be within 33–37 °C, else wait 2 s and read again; 10 out-of-range readings in a row abort;
    • both feedbacks must still read Active;
    • the amplitude readback AACT_AVGNT must track the setpoint: the ratio AACT/AREQ is calibrated over the first 3 steps, then must stay within 5 %; 5 misses in a row abort.

Check the readback check. AACT is in kV and AREQ is dimensionless (its EGU says "degrees"), so we compare their ratio, not the values. Is that how it should track?

Abort
AREQ → 0.
Done when
AREQ is at 0.53 and both feedbacks read Active.
STAGE 2

Schottky scan: emission phase

tool schottky_scan

Steps

  1. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter.
  2. Collimator at zero: if UED:USR:MMN:14:07 is more than 0.05 mm from 0, move it to 0 and wait 5 s.
  3. Diagnostic screen in (operator step).
  4. Diagnostic camera UED:GIGE:09: read 3 times with the gun UV shutter closed, then 3 times open. Continue only if an e-beam shows on top of the dark current; otherwise stop.
  5. UV throttle UED:USR:MMN:14:01 → 51°.
  6. Read the scope 3 times to set the detection threshold (5 × noise, about 7 fC).
  7. Coarse phase scan: PREQ from −180° to 175° in 5° steps, 0.5 s settle, 3 scope traces at each.
  8. The emission window is the longest run of phases above the threshold; its start is the edge.
  9. Fine scan: ±5° around the edge in 0.5° steps. The emission phase is where the charge first crosses the threshold, interpolated between points.
  10. Set PREQ → emission + 30°, wrapped to −180…180. Read 5 traces there.
  11. Save a record: throttle, PREQ, the emission phase, the charge, the scope trace and the diagnostic-camera image.

Check the emission phase is taken as the onset, where the charge first rises above 5 σ of the scope noise. Should it be the onset, the half-maximum or the peak? And is the 30° added in the direction PREQ grows?

Done when
A pulse is detected at the operating phase.
Stops for an operator
No e-beam on the diagnostic screen; no emission window above the threshold; the edge lost in the fine scan.
STAGE 3

Pulse charge and UV feedback

tool set_bunch_charge

Before it starts

  • The operator confirms Cameron's UV feedback loop is stopped: it drives the same throttle. Without that, the tool writes nothing.

Steps

  1. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter.
  2. Diagnostic screen in (operator step); gun UV shutter open.
  3. UV throttle → 53°, wait 2 s, read 5 scope traces.
  4. If the charge is outside 10 fC ± 10 %, fine-tune the throttle: first 1° (up if there is too much charge), then secant steps of at most 4°, within the throttle's safety range, up to 8 times.
  5. Read 5 traces and save a record: throttle, charge, the scope dip in mV, PREQ, the trace.
  6. Hand over to the operator to set up Cameron's loop:
    • at 10 fC (−1.7 mV) click "set low charge";
    • set 30 fC (−5.1 mV) and click "set high charge";
    • click "start feedback".
    From then on the loop owns the throttle; the agent only watches the charge (monitor_charge, read-only).

Check where Cameron's loop lives, whether it has PVs, and what "continuously tune the UV throttle to keep ___" ends with.

Check the throttle's safety range, 45–60°, is a placeholder: the IOC allows ±180°.

Done when
The charge is within 10 fC ± 10 %; then the hand-over.
STAGE 4

Beam on the YAG and the Andor EMCCD

tool beam_on_screens

Steps

  1. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter.
  2. Diagnostic screen out (operator step). Collimator at 0 mm.
  3. YAG: the e-beam check (see Every stage). It moves the cross onto the beam if the beam is near it, and stops if it is far.
  4. Full view: exposure 0.1 s, gain 0, light on.
  5. Andor: move the sample stage to andor (card out of the beam, cross in free space).
  6. Andor stop → exposure 1 s, gain 2 → start.
  7. Read the Andor 3 times; the beam must show and not saturate.

Check the run-4 check inserted the far-detector phosphor here (ASTA:BO:2114-9:BIT2). Your list has it commented out and it doesn't answer. Is a phosphor step still needed?

Done when
The e-beam is on the YAG cross (or the cross was moved onto it), and the beam is on the Andor without saturating.
Stops for an operator
No e-beam on the YAG; the e-beam far from the cross; no beam on the Andor, or saturated.
STAGE 5

Alignment through the collimator

tool align_collimator, then plan_beam_optimization

Steps

  1. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter.
  2. Move the sample stage to andor; Andor stop → exposure 1 s, gain 2 → start. Gun UV open. Collimator at 0 mm.
  3. The beam must show on the Andor (the prerequisite from stage 4); its counts are the reference with the collimator out.
  4. Insert the collimator: UED:USR:MMN:14:07 → 80, 88, then 90 mm (the 200 µm hole), reading the Andor at each. The beam should be blocked first, then reappear cut.
  5. Scan the motor over 90 ± 0.5 mm in 0.1 mm steps, Andor counts at each (3 reads, 2 s settle).
  6. Fine scan ±0.1 mm around the best point in 0.02 mm steps. Set the motor at the peak of a parabola through the best three points.
  7. Transmission = Andor counts with the collimator in ÷ counts out.

Check the insertion points (80 and 88 mm), the scan width (±0.5 mm), and the 2 % transmission we accept.

Then, the beam optimization (read-only plan for Badger)

  1. Read the Andor spot: size √(σx·σy), counts, and offset from the image centre.
  2. Build the routine:
    • variables Sol1, Sol2, XC/YC00, XC/YC01, XC/YC02, and optionally XC/YC04, each its current value ± 10 % of its safety range;
    • objective: minimize the Andor spot size;
    • constraints: Andor counts at least 0.8 × now (a proxy for the transmitted charge), and the offset from the Andor centre under 100 px when XC/YC04 are in.
    The multi-objective form maximizes the counts as a second objective instead of constraining them.
  3. An operator runs it in Badger. It escalates if it hasn't cut the spot size by 10 % within 50 evaluations.
  4. After it, check the e-beam on the YAG cross again: XC/YC01–02 should have kept the pointing there.

Check the proposal has the agent run this routine itself and escalate only if it doesn't converge. Today the agent prepares it and an operator runs it.

Check on the real machine the magnets are in BCTRL units: kG-m, or A for SOL2 and XC/YC04. Which PV sets a corrector kick, BCTRL or BDES?

Done when
Transmission is at least 2 % and the best motor position isn't at the edge of the scan.
Stops for an operator
No beam on the Andor with the collimator out; no transmission anywhere in the scan (the beam misses the hole: steer with XC/YC00).
STAGE 6

E-beam / pump-laser spatial overlap

tool overlap_pump_laser

Before it starts

  • The pump HWP angle for minimal energy, from the laser team, is in the setup file.

Steps

  1. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter.
  2. The e-beam check on the YAG (see Every stage); the cross is moved onto the e-beam if it is near.
  3. Light stays off; laser view: exposure 0.5 s, gain 5.
  4. Pump HWP UED:USR:MMN:05:02 → its minimal-energy setpoint.
  5. Pump power meter UED:USR:MMN:05:06 → 0 mm (out of the beam; it read 40 mm, in, on 2026-10-21).
  6. Gun UV shutter closed, so the laser is alone on the YAG.
  7. Open the pump shutter, UED:USR:BHC:AO1:3 → 1, and wait 3 s.
  8. Steer the laser onto the cross:
    • measure the response: pump H then V each +0.2 mm, the laser centroid after each;
    • correct at most 0.5 mm per step, up to 6 times, until within 1.5 px.
  9. Close the pump shutter. This is critical, and happens even if a step fails.
  10. Gun UV open; full view (light on).

Check "pump laser mirror H and V": we drive the run-7 list's pump lens H/V (UED:USR:MMN:08:05 / :08:06). Or is it the SmarAct UED:MCS:01:m7 / m8 ("pump_beam_X/Y")?

Check closing the gun UV during the laser view, and taking the power meter out, are our additions.

Done when
The laser is within 3 px of the cross and the e-beam is on it.
Stops for an operator
The e-beam not on the YAG, or far from the cross; the laser not seen.
STAGE 7

Sample-position tuning

tool tune_sample_position

Before it starts

  • The sample's taught position and the Andor through hole (pixel centre and radius) are in the setup file.

Steps

  1. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter. Full view.
  2. Move the sample stage to the sample (e.g. bi).
  3. Andor stop → exposure 1 s, gain 2 → start; gun UV open.
  4. Steer the main beam into the Andor's through hole with XC04/YC04 (XCOR:AS01:381, YCOR:AS01:382):
    • measure the response: each +0.2;
    • correct at most 1 per step, up to 6 times, until the main beam vanishes (Andor counts below 10 % of the start).
    Stop if it stays outside.
  5. Andor stop → exposure 2 s, gain 1000 → start (Bi). Stop, back at the low-gain settings, if the image saturates.
  6. Raster the stage around the taught position: x and y ± 0.15 mm, 7 × 7 points, 1 s settle. At each, read the total scattering intensity (Andor counts) and a pattern score where one exists.
  7. Move to the best point: intensity × score, or intensity alone when there is no score.
  8. Single crystal only: raster pitch and yaw ± 0.5°, 5 × 5, and keep the best.
  9. Save a record: position, intensity, score, the main settings (phase, amplitude, UV throttle, solenoids, collimator, pump HWP, delay, Andor exposure and gain, correctors), the Andor image and the chamber-camera image.
  10. Stop for the operator to judge the pattern. Once approved, the position is saved to the setup file (save_position).

Check the raster span (±0.15 mm) and the 1 s settle. Is total Andor intensity a fair first cut before the operator looks?

Done when
Never alone: an operator judges the pattern (ring sharpness, symmetry, one clean domain).
Stops early
The main beam not in the through hole; the Andor saturated; no diffraction anywhere in the raster.
STAGE 8

Time zero

tool find_time_zero

Before it starts

  • The pump HWP angle for ~2 µJ, from the laser group, is in the setup file.

Steps

  1. Verify the spatial overlap, without steering:
    • run the e-beam check on the YAG (the cross is not moved);
    • then the laser at minimal energy in the laser view, the power meter out, the gun UV closed, the pump shutter open;
    • the e-beam and the laser must both be within 3 px of the cross;
    • pump shutter closed, gun UV open, full view.
    If not overlapped, stop: run stage 6 again.
  2. Gate valves, then close the pump shutter.
  3. Move the sample stage to the sample (bi).
  4. Andor stop → exposure 2 s, gain 1000 → start; gun UV open.
  5. Pump HWP → its ~2 µJ setpoint; power meter out.
  6. Open the pump shutter.
  7. Delay scan on UED:USR:MMN:03:04:
    • ±3 mm in 31 points, fit an error function to the Bragg-peak drop;
    • zoom to ±0.3 mm, then ±0.03 mm, around the fitted t₀, until t₀ is known to 5 µm;
    • set the delay to t₀.
  8. Close the pump shutter, also if a step fails. Save a record.

Check on the real machine the scan runs in the LCLS DAQ or the Run 6 BOED script; the tool's scan is a stand-in. Where does the BOED script live, and which Bragg peaks does it follow? The delay's mm-to-fs calibration and its sign are also open.

Done when
t₀ is known to 5 µm and the Bragg drop is clear.
Stops for an operator
The overlap check fails; no t₀ in the coarse window.
