# device

* [Cameras](/devices/cameras.md) - The four cameras the bring-up tools read (virtual cathode, diagnostic screen, sample chamber, far-detector Andor), their image PVs and settings.
* [Collimator, diagnostic screen, scope, chamber light](/devices/beamline.md) - The collimator, the diagnostic screen (YAGS:AS01:270) and its interlock, the oscilloscope that reads the bunch charge, and the sample-chamber light.
* [Drive and pump lasers](/devices/lasers.md) - Gun UV drive (shutter, throttle) and the 400 nm pump (shutters, Harmonics HWP, ND wheel, power meter, coupling mirrors, steering lens).
* [Gate valves](/devices/vacuum.md) - The four gate valves on the e-beam path (240, 272, 280, 295) and the ePix-line valve 320.
* [Gun RF](/devices/gun-rf.md) - Gun RF on klystron KY03 (TS8): amplitude and phase setpoints and readbacks, the two feedbacks, and the gun body temperature.
* [Sample stage and pump delay](/devices/sample-and-delay.md) - The 1st (SUED) chamber sample stage and the pump long delay stage.
* [Solenoids, quads and correctors](/devices/magnets.md) - Gun solenoid (Sol1), second solenoid (Sol2), the quad doublet and the XC/YC00-05 steering correctors, with their setpoint (BCTRL) and readback (BACT) PVs.
